GiftHorse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GIFT HORSE – FZCO ("GiftHorse", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the GiftHorse mobile application, the web application and the website https://www.gift-horse.com (together, the "Services"). "You" and "your" refer to the user of the Services.

1. Who we are

The data controller is GIFT HORSE – FZCO, a free-zone company incorporated in Dubai Silicon Oasis, United Arab Emirates, under Licence No. 31611 001, registered office: IFZA Business Park, Dubai Silicon Oasis, PO Box 38252, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Contact for all privacy questions and requests: support@gift-horse.com.

2. Our principles

  • We collect only the data needed to run the Services.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use the content of your wishlists to build advertising profiles.

This Privacy Policy is part of our Terms and Conditions, available at https://www.gift-horse.com/terms-and-conditions. Please read them together.

3. Data we collect

3.1. Account data

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and, if you choose to add one, a profile photo. If you sign in with a third-party account (for example, Google or Apple), we receive the name, email address and account identifier that the provider shares with us. We do not receive your password for that account.

3.2. Content you create

We store the wishlists and wishes you create: titles, descriptions, links to products, images, prices and the privacy settings you choose for each wishlist or wish. Content you mark as public or share by link is visible to the people you share it with.

3.3. Technical and usage data

We automatically collect device type, operating system version, app version, language, IP address, crash reports and information about how you use the Services (for example, which screens you open and which features you use). We use this data to keep the Services working, fix errors and improve features.

3.4. Push notification token

If you allow notifications, we store a device token to send you notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

3.5. Cookies

Our website and web application use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for sign-in, security and basic analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some features may then stop working.

3.6. Data we do not collect

We do not access your phone contacts, precise location, photos (other than images you choose to upload), or bank card details.

4. Why we use your data and on what legal basis

  • To provide the Services — create and maintain your account, store and display your wishlists, let you share them. Basis: performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep the Services secure — prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access. Basis: our legitimate interests.
  • To analyze and improve the Services — understand which features are used and fix crashes. Basis: our legitimate interests or your consent where required by law.
  • To communicate with you — send service messages and answer your requests. Basis: performance of contract and legitimate interests.
  • To comply with the law — respond to lawful requests from authorities. Basis: legal obligation.

5. Who we share data with

5.1. Other users

Other users see only the information you choose to share: your name, profile photo and the wishlists or wishes you make public or share by link.

5.2. Service providers

We use trusted third-party providers to host our servers, store files, send notifications and emails, provide sign-in, and collect analytics and crash reports. They process data only on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes.

5.3. Online stores

When you open a product link from a wish, you go to a third-party website. That website has its own privacy policy, and we are not responsible for how it handles your data.

5.4. Legal requirements

We may disclose data if required by law, court order or a request from a competent authority, or to protect the rights and safety of GiftHorse and our users.

6. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country. When we transfer data, we use appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

7. How we protect your data

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Access to personal data is limited to authorized staff who need it for their work and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • We regularly update our software and review our security measures.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep certain data longer to comply with the law or resolve disputes. Anonymized analytics data may be kept longer.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app settings. When you delete your account, your profile, wishlists and wishes are deleted and cannot be restored. You can also request deletion by email at support@gift-horse.com. Instructions are available at https://www.gift-horse.com/how-to-delete.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your personal data;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To use these rights, email us at support@gift-horse.com. We respond within 30 days.

11. Children

The Services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The new version takes effect when we publish it at https://www.gift-horse.com/privacy-policy. If the changes are significant, we will notify you in the app or by email.

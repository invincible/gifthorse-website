QUESTIONS ABOUT GIFTHORSE

Good to
know

Adding wishes, sharing lists
and checking prices.

Can I add gifts from different stores?+

Yes. Save product links from different online stores in one place. Use your browser’s Share menu on iOS or Android, paste a link, or add a wish manually with a photo and notes. Details imported from websites can vary.

How do I share my wishlist?+

Share your wishlist link with friends and family in a message, a group chat or your social bio.

Do prices update automatically?+

No. The price and currency are taken from the store once when you add the item. GiftHorse does not track price changes. Check the store for the current price and availability before buying.

Does GiftHorse sell the gifts?+

GiftHorse brings your wishes together. Purchases happen at the original store, where you can check shipping, returns, current prices and availability.

Where can I get GiftHorse?+

GiftHorse is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play. Choose your store below to get started.
